
# [Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce](https://luyennoi.com/question-answer/PART%202~Describe%20an%20environmental%20law%20you%20would%20like%20your%20country%20to%20introduce)

I'd like to talk about **a law that requires** all new buildings to be environmentally friendly, or what people often call green buildings.

I first heard about this idea when I was still in university. At first, I didn't care about it because I thought it was just a boring lesson. However, when my teacher showed me a video about green roof buildings, I became more and more interested. It wasn't just about the environmental benefits, but it also helped the buildings' appearance look beautiful. The idea of covering the roof with plants not only helps improve the air quality, but also creates a relaxing space for people living there.

To make this idea happen, the law would require new buildings to have **solar panels and rooftop gardens** where people can grow vegetables or fruit trees. These plants also help cool down the building in summer, which saves a lot of electricity. Although the building costs are higher at first, the energy savings and comfortable space make it totally worth the money.

I'd really like my country to introduce this law because air pollution is a pressing issue in major cities. I believe green buildings can definitely make our cities cleaner and healthier to live in. Plus, it would encourage everyone to care more about the environment.
