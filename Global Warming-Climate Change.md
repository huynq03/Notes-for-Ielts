### Some people think that instead of preventing climate change, we need to find a way to live with it. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

General Statement
	In recent decades, global warming has been receiving a great deal of media attention around the world due to its substantial impacts on human life
	Tự viết :
		**In this day and age**, global warming **has become a problem of great concern to the general public** due to it's **substantial impacts on** human life.
Opinion
	Although – cấu trúc ưa thích cho câu Opinion
	**Although** the idea of coexistence between climate change and humans appears reasonable, this cannot be viewed as the ultimate approach for humankind to combat climate change. => **I disagree
	- Live with it = co-exist 
	- Prevent climate change = combat climate change 
	- Climate change = global warming 
	- Some people believe that = appears reasonable
	Tự viết
		 Although the idea of coexistence between climate change and humans appears reasonable, this is not the best way for us to deal with climate change.
Topic sentence 1
	**There are a number of reasons why** the coexistence can ***do more harm than good.***
	1. is fraught with pitfalls -> is bad 
	2. can have many downsides -> is bad
Supporting idea 1.1
	**First**, climate change is largely shaped by human caused geoengineering rather than nature itself.
Example/Result 1.1
	**For example**, in order to produce the energy that drives the world’s economy, most nations rely on carbon-rich fuels like coal, oil and gas, **which directly correlates with the intensification of global warming.**(đoạn này là result, lấy ví dụ phải có kết quả)
	VERSION SIMPLE:
	For example, most nations rely on fossel fuels like coal, oil and gas, greatly contributing to global warming.
Supporting idea 1.2
	**Second**, even if citizens agree to keep the environment the way it is, corporations, especially in the manufacturing and mining industries, would not as that would affect their profits.
	• Even if – Rất mạnh, giúp làm nổi bật vấn đề. 
	• Especially -> Giúp bạn đi sâu vào chi tiết một cách khéo léo. (Phải có 2 dấu phẩy kẹp ở giữa.)
Example/Result 1.2
	Therefore, it can be implied that if corporations were not held accountable for their contribution to global warming as in the case of coexistence, the issue of climate change would inevitably be exacerbated.

##### Topic sentence 2 
	It is more economically sound to actively prevent climate change than to ignore it.
Supporting idea 2.1 
	In fact, the costs, either financial or non-financial, of dealing with future consequences of climate change can be overwhelming. 
Example result 2.1 
	For instance, the increase in intensity and frequency of storms can cause massive destructions and deaths in many coastal areas, which would take decades to recover.
Supporting idea 2.2 
	In addition, rising temperatures have also contributed to the extinction of species worldwide. 
Example result 2.2 
	It should be noted that the extinction is irreversible and comes at great cost to the ecosystem and apparently humans living within it.
Conclusion - restatement 
	In conclusion, actively combating global warming must be regarded as top priority given the high urgency.
