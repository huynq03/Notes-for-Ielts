**Some people think that in order to reduce traffic congestion, the ONLY way is to increase fuel prices.

DISAGREE : higher fuel prices may help, but they are not the only solution.

Intro 2 câu
**Body 1: Fuel prices alone are insufficient**
- **commuters still need cars**
    - rely on private vehicles for work and daily travel
    - continue driving despite higher fuel costs
- **higher-income drivers are less affected**
    - can easily afford more expensive petrol
    - may not change their driving habits
    - therefore, the number of cars on the roads may remain high
Từ vựng nên học:  
**commuters, rely on private vehicles, daily travel, higher fuel costs, higher-income motorists, afford, driving habits, remain high, reduce traffic volume**

**Body 2: Other policies can be more effective**
- **expand and improve public transport networks**
    - affordable, frequent and reliable services
    - encourage commuters to switch to buses or trains
    - reduce reliance on private vehicles
- **introduce congestion charges / car-free zones**
    - make unnecessary car journeys less attractive
    - reduce traffic in busy city centres
Từ vựng liên quan:  
**expand public transport networks, reliable services, affordable fares, public transit, switch to buses and trains, reduce reliance on private vehicles, congestion charges, car-free zones, discourage unnecessary journeys, busy city centres, alleviate congestion**


### Body 1 — Fuel prices alone are insufficient

Ý 1 rất ổn:

- **commuters still need cars**
    - rely on private vehicles for work and daily travel
    - continue driving despite higher fuel costs

Ý 2 cũng tốt:

- **higher-income drivers are less affected**
    - can easily afford more expensive petrol
    - may not change their driving habits
    - therefore, the number of cars on the roads may remain high

Chỗ này bạn có thể dùng `reduce traffic volume`, nhưng trong bài IELTS tự nhiên hơn là:

> **reduce the number of vehicles on the roads**

hoặc

> **reduce the volume of traffic**

`traffic volume` đúng, nhưng hơi technical hơn mức bạn cần.


### Body 2 — Other policies can be more effective

Phần này cũng tốt, thậm chí mình nghĩ **mạnh hơn Body 1**.

Ý 1:

- **expand and improve public transport networks**
    - affordable, frequent and reliable services
    - encourage commuters to switch to buses or trains
    - reduce reliance on private vehicles

Rất logic:

> better public transport  
> → people have an alternative  
> → fewer cars  
> → less congestion

Ý 2:

- **introduce congestion charges / car-free zones**
    - make unnecessary car journeys less attractive
    - reduce traffic in busy city centres

Cũng rất trực tiếp với đề.



### Vocab nên ưu tiên học

Danh sách của bạn tốt, nhưng nếu mục tiêu là 6.5–7 thì mình sẽ **ưu tiên học chắc khoảng 12 cụm này**, thay vì cố nhồi hết:

- `traffic congestion`
- `commuters`
- `rely on private vehicles`
- `higher fuel costs`
- `higher-income motorists`
- `afford the additional cost`
- `driving habits`
- `public transport networks`
- `affordable and reliable services`
- `switch to buses and trains`
- `reduce reliance on private vehicles`
- `congestion charges`
- `car-free zones`

Đặc biệt, 3 chuỗi collocation rất đáng học nguyên cụm:

> **rely on private vehicles → reduce reliance on private vehicles**
