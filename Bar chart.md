- **Article: a / an / the**
    - ❌ a 8-year period
    - ✅ **an 8-year period**
    - ❌ among four cities
    - ✅ among **the** four cities
- **Plural nouns**
    - ❌ Australian spent
    - ✅ **Australians** spent
    - ❌ Adelaide resident
    - ✅ Adelaide **residents**
- **Possessive ’s**
    - ❌ Perth' travel time
    - ✅ **Perth’s** travel time
- **BY vs TO khi mô tả số liệu**
    - **increase by + amount**
    - **increase to + final number**
    - ✅ increased **by 3 minutes**
    - ✅ increased **to 28 minutes**
- **Adjective vs adverb**
    - ❌ declined negligible
    - ✅ declined **negligibly**
    - hoặc tự nhiên hơn: declined **slightly**
    - Nhưng: a **slight** decline
- **Linking clauses**
    - ❌ Sydney increased, meanwhile Adelaide...
    - ✅ Sydney increased, **whereas** Adelaide...
    - ✅ Sydney increased. **Meanwhile,** Adelaide...
