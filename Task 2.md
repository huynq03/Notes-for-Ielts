## How to write an introduction

**Sentence 1:** Paraphrase the topic.  
**Sentence 2:** Directly answer the question / state your position.

### 1. Opinion Essay

**S1:** It is sometimes argued/believed that **[paraphrased opinion]**.  
**S2:** I completely agree/disagree with this view because **[reason 1]** and **[reason 2]**.
### 2. Discussion Essay

**S1:** People have different views about whether **[view A]** or **[view B]**.  
 
Some people believe that **[view A]**, while others argue that **[view B]**. 
**S2:** While/ Although **[view A has some merits]**, I believe that **[view B is better/ are more significant]** because **[main reason]**.

Alternative:

> Some people believe that **[view A]**, while others argue that **[view B]**. Although **[concession]**, I am of the opinion that **[your opinion]**.

### 3. Problem/Cause–Solution Essay

**S1:** It is true that **[paraphrased problem]**.  
**S2:** There are several reasons/problems behind this situation, but **[governments/individuals]** could take various measures to address it.

Other forms:

> This issue is caused by **[general causes]**, and several effective solutions can be implemented.

> This development creates several problems, although they can be addressed through appropriate measures.

### 4. Two-part Question Essay

**S1:** Paraphrase the main topic.  
**S2:** There are several reasons why **[answer to question 1]**.  
**S3:** In my opinion, **[direct answer to question 2]**.

Combined version:

> This trend occurs for several reasons, and I believe that it is **[positive/negative/beneficial/harmful]** overall.
