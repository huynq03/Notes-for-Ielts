## Photography
- Do you like taking photographs?
Absolutely yes! I think taking photos is a good way to **capture memorable moments**, and I really like taking **landscape photos**. When I'm in my hometown, I usually go around to take pictures of the mountains and the river near my house

- How often do you take photos?
I take photos quite often, almost every day, because using a smartphone is **a very easy way** to do it. **Whenever** I see **beautiful scenery** or delicious food, I will **snap a quick photo** to **capture the moment**

- Do you prefer to take photographs yourself or to have other people take photos?
 Well, it depends on the circumstances/situation. If I'm out alone, I usually just **take a selfie**. However, when I'm with my friends, I prefer asking them to **take photos of me**. That way, I can get a **full-body shot** that includes the **landscape in the background**
 - Do you prefer to take pictures of people or of scenery?
I definitely prefer taking photos of **scenery**. I love exploring different places and enjoying nature. Whenever I come across a **breathtaking view**, I immediately **snap a photo** with my phone.
