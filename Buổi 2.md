Walking
Do you like walking?
Do you like to walk on your own or with others?
Would you say that your city is a good place for walking?

Definitely! I'm **extremely keen on** walking. In the evening, after a long day of working or studying, I usually take a walk in **Thống Nhất Park** . It's a fantastic way to **blow off some steam** and **recharge my battery**.

Well, it really depends on my mood. When I need to **recharge my battery**/ need a little bit of "me" time and think about your **life**, I prefer walking on my own around **Thống Nhất Park**.  But when I want to **catch up with my friends**, we usually **set up a walk together** to chat.

- definitely prefer walking with friends or my relatives
- able to chat with them about everything under the sun
- a good way to stay physically fit while bonding with that person.
- however, walking alone is a good idea if you really need a little bit of "me" time and think about your life.

Well, now that I'm living in **Ho Chi Minh City**, I would say **it's a really good place for walking** because **there are plenty of great spots**, like **Nguyễn Huệ Walking Street**. Walking there in the evening is very enjoyable and helps me **get some fresh air**.
